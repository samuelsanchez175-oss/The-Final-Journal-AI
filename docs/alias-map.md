# Penwork Artist Alias Map

Built 2026-10-10 from `BarHitsCatalog.sqlite` (read-only), artists with 20+ distinct songs. Each entry has the same fields in `alias-map.json`.

## How to score a bar

1. Exact stage name or unique alias (e.g. *Thugger*, *Rozay*, *Hov*, *Glizock*) = strong hit.
2. A **collision alias** (an everyday word or shared name, listed under Collisions) only counts when a **cue toward** appears on the same line or the next one.
3. Any **cue away** on the line cancels the match, or moves it to the matching character/person fold.
4. When two artists share an alias (Pluto, Carter, Glo, Slime, Wayne), the one with more cues wins. With no cues, leave the bar unfiled.
5. Label, group and producer rows (Quality Control, Young Stoner Life, Sniper Gang, YUME, Migos) aren't artists. Re-file those bars under whoever performs them.
