# Nouns from the NoCap lines

NoCap is finished: 290 songs, 1,411 community notes opened. Drake is in progress in Chrome.
A chip is a word the lyric says, in both the store and the section. A name that only appears in the note is not a chip.
A Sound flip is the phrase on the bar. The partner is a second word on that same bar. The next bar does not count.
Sentence scraps such as "I'm Quagmire" and "These Percocets" were dropped. The noun is the name after that scrap.
