# Ballout

Simile literalization, phase 1. Exact artist string only. The vault was opened read-only.

Bars in the vault: 1,034. Songs: 28. Like/as lines opened: 74.

Candidates: 5. HIGH: 4. MED: 1.

A candidate is a bar where a slang or figurative word is compared with like or as to a concrete object, brand, or physical thing, and that object flips the word. The comparison is the punchline. Filler like, person comparisons, and same-sense similes were left out. The nine example bars in the job were not in this extract.

## HIGH

1. **Different (Remix)**
   - Pour up a pint, yeah I'm high as a kite
   - Flip: high → kite. High from the pint is re-anchored to a kite in the air.

2. **Different (Remix)**
   - Water looking like some voss, money falling out the vault
   - Flip: water → Voss. Water (diamond clarity) is re-anchored to the bottled-water brand.

3. **Ring Bells**
   - Sippin on a brick and I'm posted like a wall
   - Flip: posted → wall. Posted (standing on the block) is re-anchored to a wall. The brick in the same bar locks the masonry.

4. **Wheel**
   - I'm rollin' hard like I'm a Ferris wheel
   - Flip: rollin' → Ferris wheel. Rollin' is re-anchored to the wheel that rolls.

## MED

1. **Spyder**
   - Lookin' like an icy cone
   - Flip: icy → icy cone. Icy is re-anchored to a snow cone. The bar does not say diamonds.
