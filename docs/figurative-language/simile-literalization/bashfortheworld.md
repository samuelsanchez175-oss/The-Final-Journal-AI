# BashfortheWorld

Simile literalization, phase 1. Exact artist string only. The vault was opened read-only.

Bars in the vault: 1,943. Songs: 55. Like/as lines opened: 110.

Candidates: 7. HIGH: 5. MED: 2.

A candidate is a bar where a slang or figurative word is compared with like or as to a concrete object, brand, or physical thing, and that object flips the word. The comparison is the punchline. Filler like, person comparisons, and same-sense similes were left out. The nine example bars in the job were not in this extract.

## HIGH

1. **Rich Migos**
   - Nigga, you ain’t did none, pipe like a big plumber
   - Flip: pipe → plumber. Pipe (the gun) is re-anchored to a plumber's pipe.

2. **Vida**
   - Still gon' lean like the Pisa (Ay)
   - Flip: lean → Leaning Tower of Pisa. Lean (the drank) is re-anchored to the tower that leans.

3. **RIP Trayvon**
   - Still keep the jack on point like a needle
   - Flip: on point → needle. On point (excellent) is re-anchored to the point of a needle.

4. **Fireworks**
   - Ima pop my shit every day like firework
   - Flip: pop → firework. Pop my shit is re-anchored to a firework going off.

5. **Heavy**
   - I'm swipin' my visa, I viva la vida, got stripes like Adidas
   - Flip: stripes → Adidas. Stripes (rank) are re-anchored to the stripes on the shoe.
