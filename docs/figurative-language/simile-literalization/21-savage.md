# 21 Savage

Simile literalization, phase 1. Exact artist string only. The vault was opened read-only.

Bars in the vault: 9,417. Songs: 234. Like/as lines opened: 618.

Candidates: 49. HIGH: 45. MED: 4.

A candidate is a bar where a slang or figurative word is compared with like or as to a concrete object, brand, or physical thing, and that object flips the word. The comparison is the punchline. Filler like, person comparisons, and same-sense similes were left out. The nine example bars in the job were not in this extract.
